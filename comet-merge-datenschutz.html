Comet Merge – Privacy Policy

Comet Merge works completely offline and does not collect personal data. Game progress, leaderboard, achievements, statistics and settings are stored only on your device and are removed when you delete the app. There are no ads, no analytics and no account. If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device; no data is transmitted.
